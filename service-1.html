Landscape Design & Planning

A truly beautiful garden is more than just a collection of plants; it's a personal oasis, a tranquil escape crafted just for you. Here in the heart of Kolkata, we see every outdoor area not just for what it is, but for what it can become. Our passion is to partner with you, turning your unique vision into a living, breathing reality that you can cherish for years. Our process begins with listening. We take your ideas and blend them with our deep, practical understanding of the local climate, soil, and native flora. This ensures that your garden is not only stunning but also sustainable and built to thrive. We believe in a collaborative journey, creating a perfect harmony of beauty and practicality that results in a stunning, enduring outdoor space designed for living.

Custom Landscape Design

Your garden should be as unique as you are. We work with you to create a personalized design that reflects your style, needs, and the specific character of your property, ensuring a beautiful and functional outdoor sanctuary.

Landscape Architecture

For larger or more complex projects, our architectural services provide a comprehensive plan. We address grading, drainage, structures, and planting with technical precision, ensuring a seamless and sustainable integration with the environment.

Garden Design

We specialize in creating stunning garden compositions. From vibrant flower beds and lush foliage combinations to themed gardens, we focus on the art of planting to bring color, texture, and life to your outdoor space.

Complete Yard Designs

We design your entire yard to be a cohesive and functional space for your family. This includes planning for lawns, patios, walkways, and play areas, ensuring every corner of your property is both beautiful and usable.
